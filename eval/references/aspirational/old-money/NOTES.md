# Old Money / Monaco — reference set (founder-supplied, 2026-08-25)

15 Monaco/Riviera references that drove the `old-money` pack. Most are real phone
photos; **103** (villa staircase at sunset) is an AI-render — composition/mood only,
never the realism-quality set.

## Settings (the real signifiers)
- **Place du Casino / Casino de Monte-Carlo** (101, 104, 109): Belle-Époque casino,
  a row of parked supercars (red/yellow Ferraris), the Hôtel de Paris, valets in vests.
  Warm-lit and grand — the hero location, day or night.
- **Grand-Prix hairpin by the Fairmont** (104, 108): red-and-white kerbs, a Ferrari
  mid-corner; people walking the track road.
- **Belle-Époque streets** (95, 97, 98): cream/pastel-yellow facades, wrought-iron
  balconies, red-and-white Monaco flags, palms, the grey Tête-de-Chien cliff behind,
  cobbles, scooters, a Smart car.
- **Fontvieille harbour from above** (99, 102, 107): rows of white yachts on deep teal
  water, terracotta apartment blocks, green headland; blue-hour glow on the boats.
- **Luxury-boutique frontage** (96, 100): Tiffany, Ferragamo, Graff — a Rolls-Royce and
  a Porsche parked outside.

## Wardrobe (validates the two-fit + adds the night look)
- **Day (the signature Monaco menswear):** pale-blue or white **linen shirt / polo** +
  **white or cream wide-leg pleated trousers** + loafers + watch + sunglasses (107, 108),
  or a blue polo + grey tailored trousers (105).
- **Night:** a **black silky shirt + white pleated trousers** by a classic car outside
  the lit Casino (109) — elegant, cinematic.
- Grey linen overshirt + cream trousers at a Como-style iron gate (106).
- All fully clothed; no swimwear.

## Poses
Walking the hairpin (108), leaning on a railing over the harbor (107), by a supercar
adjusting a watch, standing beside a classic car at the Casino at night (109), at an
iron gate looking out (106). Candid, turned, between-takes — never straight-on to camera.

## Light
Day: bright hazy Mediterranean sun, deep-blue sky, hard clean shadows, hazy headland.
Night: warm glowing facade lights + lanterns against a blue-hour sky, reflections on
car paint. Muted elegant grade with the occasional bold supercar red/yellow.
