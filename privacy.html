Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026 · Effective: August 23, 2026

Gems helps you find, edit, and share the best photos already on your phone. This policy explains what we collect, how your photos are handled, and the choices you have. The short version: your photos live on your device, and we try to send as little as possible off it.

The one-paragraph summary. Your original photos stay on your device. To analyze them, Gems creates small preview images and sends those to our AI provider to describe what makes a photo good — it does not keep your library. Your full-size photo is only ever sent when you ask for a specific edit. We do not sell your data, we do not build face-recognition profiles, and you can delete your account and data at any time.

1. Who we are

Gems (“Gems,” “we,” “us”) provides the Gems app and website. If you have any question about this policy or your data, contact us at privacy@gems.app.

2. Who can use Gems

Gems is intended for people 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, contact privacy@gems.app and we will delete it.

If you are between 13 and 17, please use Gems with a parent or guardian’s permission. For users under 18, we minimize what we collect: we do not store your gender, and we do not use your activity to train models.

3. How your photos are handled

This is the most important part, so we’re specific:

  • Your originals stay on your device. Imported photos are stored locally on your phone (in the app’s on-device storage). We do not upload your photo library to our servers.
  • Analysis uses small previews. To rank and describe your photos, Gems creates downscaled preview images (roughly 512 pixels on the longest edge) and sends those to our AI provider (Google’s Gemini API). These previews are used to generate a description and are not used by us to build a stored copy of your library.
  • Full-resolution photos leave only for an edit you request. When you ask Gems to edit a specific photo (for example, “make it brighter” or “remove the object in the background”), that one photo is sent to the AI provider to produce the edit. Tonal edits like brightness or contrast are applied on your device and are not sent anywhere.
  • Edited results are saved privately. Photos you save after editing are stored in a private, per-user storage area that only your account can access.
  • No biometric or face-recognition data. We do not create or store facial-recognition templates, face embeddings, or other biometric identifiers on our servers.

4. Information we collect

You give us

  • Account: your email address (for sign-in) and the display name you choose.
  • Onboarding preferences: your age range and the aesthetics/styles you pick, so Gems can tailor suggestions. (Gender, if provided, is not stored for users under 18.)
  • Content: projects, edits, and moodboards you create in the app.

Created as you use Gems

  • Activity signals (“taste events”): in-app actions such as which photos you keep, rank, or edit. We use these to improve your recommendations. You can turn off using this activity to train models (see Your choices); it is off by default.
  • Photo descriptions: text descriptions of your photos generated during analysis, stored with the photo on your device and used to rank them.
  • Subscription status if you subscribe to Gems Plus (managed through the app store; we do not receive your full card details).
  • Basic technical data needed to run the service securely (e.g. authentication tokens, error logs). We do not use third-party advertising trackers.

5. How we use your information

  • To provide Gems: import, rank, edit, organize, and export your photos.
  • To personalize suggestions based on the aesthetics and choices you make.
  • To operate your account, authenticate you, and process a Gems Plus subscription.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse, and fix problems.
  • To improve features. We only use your activity to train models if you have opted in, and never for users under 18.

6. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share only with service providers that help us run Gems:

  • Google (Gemini API) — processes photo previews (and full photos for edits you request) to generate descriptions and edits. Your data is sent for processing to return a result.
  • Supabase — our backend provider for authentication, database, and private storage of your account data and saved edits.
  • Google Sign-In — if you choose to sign in with Google, Google authenticates you and shares your basic profile/email with us.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of our users or Gems.

7. Security

Your account data is protected with per-user access controls so that only you can read your own data, and it is encrypted in transit. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and limit what leaves your device.

8. How long we keep it

We keep account data while your account is active. Photos and edits stored on your device remain until you delete them or remove the app. When you delete your account, we delete the account data and saved edits associated with it.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Access and delete: you can delete your account and associated data from within the app (Profile → Privacy & data). This removes your account data and saved edits.
  • Training consent: using your activity to improve models is off by default; you can leave it off or turn it on at any time.
  • Local photos: you can remove imported photos from the app’s on-device storage at any time.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port your data, or to object to certain processing. To exercise these, contact privacy@gems.app.

10. International users

Gems and its providers may process data in the United States and other countries. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for cross-border transfers.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Gems evolves. If we make material changes, we will update the date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.

12. Contact

Questions or requests: privacy@gems.app.
